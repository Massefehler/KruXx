Playback:
- Opening an album and tapping a track now plays the whole album in its own order; the automatic queue top-up extends the queue instead of replacing it
- Keeps every song of the current queue when an explicit radio replaces it, and no longer adds related songs twice
- Starts the track you actually tapped, even when a list contains the same video twice
- Measures the remaining queue in playback order, so turning shuffle on no longer pulls in a radio right away
- Appends topped-up songs behind the queue under shuffle as well, so a shuffled album finishes before other artists follow

Player:
- The shuffle button in the player's action bar now switches the same shuffle mode as the media notification and Android Auto, and shows whether it is on; it previously reordered the queue once, dropped the play history and was always drawn as active
- Gives every action of the bar the full 48 dp touch target while keeping the icon size, so a tap next to an icon no longer opens the queue instead
